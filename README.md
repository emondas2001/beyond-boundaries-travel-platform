# Beyond Boundaries

**Beyond Boundaries** is a comprehensive web platform designed for tourists and travelers. The platform helps users explore destinations, find hotels, hire guides, visit restaurants, rent vehicles, and access essential services like hospitals, all from a single dashboard. The goal of the project is to make traveling and planning trips simple, efficient, and enjoyable.

---

## Project Overview

Beyond Boundaries provides the following functionality:

- **User Authentication:** Users can register, login, and manage their account securely.
- **Dashboard:** Users have a personalized dashboard to view bookings and manage preferences.
- **Explore Places:** Users can discover tourist destinations, view details, and save places to a wishlist.
- **Hotel Booking Module:** Users can view available hotels, check room details and prices, and book rooms for selected dates.
- **Guides:** The platform provides information about local guides, including their ratings, experience, and price.
- **Restaurants:** Users can find restaurants, view menu and food prices, and place orders.
- **Vehicles:** Users can book or rent cars, bikes, buses, and jeeps, along with driver details, experience, and ratings.
- **Hospitals:** The platform suggests nearby hospitals, allows doctor appointment booking, and provides ambulance service information.
- **Settings, About, and Contact:** Users can update their profile, view contact information, and learn about the platform.

The project is designed to integrate multiple travel-related services in one platform to simplify trip planning for tourists.

## Future Goals

The project is designed to grow into a complete travel platform. Planned enhancements include:

1. **Ratings & Reviews System:** Users can rate and review hotels, guides, restaurants, vehicles, and tourist places.  
2. **Trip Planner Module:** Users can plan full trips, including destinations, hotels, guides, and vehicles for selected dates.  
3. **Payment Integration:** Online payment system for bookings of hotels, restaurants, and vehicles.  
4. **Notifications & Reminders:** Alerts for bookings, appointments, and trip start dates.  
5. **Weather & Travel Advisory:** Display weather information for destinations.  
6. **Admin / Business Panel:** Allow hotel owners, restaurants, guides, and vehicle providers to manage their listings.  
7. **Multi-language Support:** Expand accessibility for international travelers.  
8. **Map Integration:** Embed Google Maps for locations such as hotels, restaurants, hospitals, and tourist spots.
